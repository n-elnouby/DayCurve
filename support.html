Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. DayCurve has no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes its data; caffeine you saved to Apple Health stays in the Health app.

How does DayCurve know how much caffeine is left?

It uses a standard model: caffeine is absorbed over about 45 minutes and then halves roughly every 5 hours. You can adjust the half-life in Settings (smoking, the pill and pregnancy change it). It is an estimate, not a measurement.

What is the cut-off?

The latest time you can have your usual drink and still be at or under your sleep-safe level (50 mg by default) at bedtime.

A drink has the wrong amount.

Caffeine content varies by country and recipe. You can log a custom amount for any drink, and tell us so we can correct the catalog.

My chronotype says "from your answers".

Turn on Apple Health in Settings. After about ten nights of sleep data, DayCurve uses your real sleep instead of the questionnaire.

Is the melatonin timing medical advice?

No. DayCurve gives general information based on published research. Melatonin rules differ by country; talk to a doctor before using it, especially if you are pregnant, take medication or have a sleep disorder. You can turn melatonin suggestions off in Settings.

How do I cancel DayCurve Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. A lifetime purchase or trip pass can be restored with "Restore purchases" in DayCurve's Settings.
