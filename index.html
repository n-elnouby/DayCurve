DayCurve: Caffeine & Sleep

One clock for caffeine, light and sleep — for iPhone and Apple Watch.

DayCurve logs your caffeine, shows how much is still active, and times coffee, light and sleep around your own body clock — at home and across time zones.

  • Caffeine log with major brands, barcode scanner and custom drinks
  • Live caffeine curve, your daily cut-off and what is left at bedtime
  • Chronotype from a short questionnaire, refined from Apple Health sleep
  • Daily windows for caffeine, light, wind-down and sleep
  • Jet-lag plans, hour by hour
  • Apple Watch app, complications, widgets and a Live Activity
